# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Health Prediction System Using Machine Learning

## Our Pledge

As part of the Health Prediction System project, we aim to create an open, welcoming, and collaborative environment where developers, students, researchers, and machine learning enthusiasts can contribute, learn, and share ideas.

We are committed to ensuring that participation in this project remains respectful and free from harassment or discrimination, regardless of age, gender, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, educational background, technical experience, or personal identity.

Our goal is to encourage collaboration while promoting responsible and ethical applications of artificial intelligence, particularly in healthcare-related projects.

## Our Standards

We encourage all contributors to maintain a positive and professional environment.

Examples of acceptable behavior include:

- Using respectful, inclusive, and welcoming language when communicating with other contributors.
- Respecting different viewpoints, experiences, and technical approaches.
- Providing constructive feedback on code, machine learning models, and project documentation.
- Helping beginners understand programming concepts and machine learning techniques.
- Acknowledging the contributions and ideas of other developers.
- Encouraging responsible development and transparent evaluation of AI systems.
- Respecting data privacy and avoiding the unauthorized sharing of personal or medical information.
- Working collaboratively to improve the project's functionality, reliability, and accessibility.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, bullying, discrimination, or personal attacks against other contributors.
- Offensive, insulting, or derogatory language directed toward individuals or communities.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately introducing malicious code, misleading information, or harmful functionality.
- Misrepresenting machine learning predictions as medically verified diagnoses.
- Sharing sensitive patient information or confidential healthcare records without authorization.
- Plagiarizing code, research, or documentation without appropriate acknowledgment.
- Disrupting project discussions or deliberately preventing constructive collaboration.

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for establishing and maintaining standards of respectful behavior within the repository.

Maintainers are expected to:

- Review contributions fairly and provide constructive feedback.
- Encourage collaboration between contributors with different levels of experience.
- Address inappropriate behavior professionally and consistently.
- Maintain transparency regarding project limitations and machine learning performance.
- Promote responsible handling of healthcare-related datasets.
- Ensure that project documentation accurately represents the system's capabilities.

Maintainers reserve the right to edit, reject, or remove contributions, comments, issues, or pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct.

Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from participating in the project.

## Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence

Because this project explores machine learning applications in healthcare, contributors are expected to follow responsible AI development practices.

All contributors should:

- Clearly communicate the limitations of machine learning predictions.
- Avoid making unsupported claims about medical accuracy or reliability.
- Use synthetic, anonymized, or appropriately authorized datasets.
- Respect the privacy and confidentiality of health-related information.
- Encourage proper testing and evaluation before proposing model improvements.
- Avoid presenting the application as a replacement for qualified healthcare professionals.

**The Health Prediction System is intended strictly for educational purposes and must not be represented as a clinically validated medical diagnostic tool.**

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project-related spaces, including:

- GitHub repository discussions and issues.
- Pull requests and code reviews.
- Project documentation and contributions.
- Online discussions related to the project.
- Public interactions where individuals represent the project or its community.

All contributors are expected to follow these standards when participating in activities associated with the Health Prediction System.

## Enforcement

As the sole developer and maintainer of the Health Prediction System, I am responsible for ensuring that this repository remains a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative environment.

Any instances of harassment, discrimination, inappropriate behavior, or violations of this Code of Conduct will be reviewed and addressed by me in a fair and responsible manner.

Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, appropriate actions may include:

- Issuing a warning and requesting corrective action.
- Removing inappropriate comments, issues, or contributions.
- Rejecting pull requests that violate the project's guidelines.
- Temporarily restricting individuals from participating in project discussions.
- Permanently restricting contributors who repeatedly engage in harmful or unacceptable behavior.

I am committed to handling concerns fairly, maintaining confidentiality where possible, and ensuring that all contributors are treated respectfully.

Any concerns regarding violations of this Code of Conduct can be reported directly to me shreyan.jain0301\@gmail.com.


## Educational Purpose

The Health Prediction System was developed to demonstrate the practical application of machine learning, data preprocessing, and predictive analytics.

Contributions should support the educational objectives of the project and encourage responsible experimentation with artificial intelligence.

The application is not intended to diagnose, treat, or prevent medical conditions, and contributors must avoid making claims suggesting otherwise.
